Steamworks runtime redistributables

steam_api64.dll and libsteam_api.so are Valve Corporation's Steamworks SDK
runtime redistributables, supplied with the official GodotSteam 4.23.2 release
(Steamworks SDK 1.65). They are not covered by GodotSteam's MIT license.

The Windows redistributable's original version-resource notice reads:
CompanyName: Valve Corporation
LegalCopyright: Copyright (C) 2007

Use and redistribution of Valve's runtime are governed by the applicable
Steamworks SDK and Steam distribution agreements. This attribution does not
replace those agreements or grant independent rights to redistribute the SDK.

Official SDK and runtime distribution documentation:
https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/sdk
https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/sdk/api

The MIT notice for the separate GodotSteam wrapper is in license.md.
Exact binary provenance and hashes are recorded in dependency-lock.json.
