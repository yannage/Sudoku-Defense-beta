Prepared October 24, 2026. These claims describe the current Godot build. The alpha uses a free Playtest; the main game remains unreleased. Do not label this build Steam Deck Verified or promise a release date.

## Short description

Solve Sudoku to grow towers, then defend the board. Your answers shape the enemy route. Stack loot on your towers, upgrade whole numbers, and risk an illegal move when a wave gets through. A puzzle and tower defense roguelite built around one changing grid.

## About This Game — English

[h2]Every answer is a tower.[/h2]

Sudoku Defense turns a Sudoku puzzle into a battlefield. Fill an empty square with the right number to grow a tower and earn credits. When you're ready, prepare your defenses and let the wave begin.

[h2]Solve first. Fight next.[/h2]

Your recent placements shape the enemy route. Use the solve phase to build a strong position, then watch the board become a voxel battlefield. Need time to think? Open your gear to pause the action, or speed up a wave to 2× or 4×.

[h2]Build the tower you need.[/h2]

Each tower has five module slots. Stack lightning, frost, splash damage, faster shots, and other effects—or mix them. Drag gear onto a tower, swap a module, or upgrade every tower with the same number at once. Number highlights show which towers an upgrade will affect.

<!-- Insert gear-attachment loop here. -->

[h2]Break a rule. Take a risk.[/h2]

During a wave, a reserve seed lets you place an emergency tower—even if its number breaks Sudoku rules. That extra firepower comes with corruption, which gives later enemies more health. Decide whether the board can hold without it.

[h2]Open chests. Keep building.[/h2]

Defeated enemies drop fragments and sometimes chests. Spend fragments to open them, then equip your new modules or save a temporary boost for a difficult wave. Between rounds, spend credits on upgrades or try the salvage slots. If a spin returns fewer credits than it costs, you also get a common module.

Play with a controller, mouse, or touchscreen. Runs save locally so you can come back later.
