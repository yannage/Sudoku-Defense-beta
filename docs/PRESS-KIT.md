# Sudoku Defense — press kit

Prepared October 24, 2026. This is a development factsheet, not a launch announcement.

**Sudoku Defense turns correct Sudoku answers into towers.** Solve part of a
puzzle, equip the towers you have grown, then defend against a wave of enemies.
Your recent placements help shape their route. Between battles, open chests,
collect gear, and decide how to spend your credits before the next round.

## What players do

- **Solve to build:** each correct placement plants a voxel seed, reveals the number, and grows a tower while earning credits.
- **Shape the defense:** recent Sudoku moves influence the route enemies take.
- **Make a build:** equip up to five modules per tower, combining effects such as rapid fire, electricity, and slow.
- **Collect and upgrade:** open voxel chests, find gear, and upgrade one tower or every tower with the same number.
- **Choose a run:** play one round in Free Play or attempt up to fifty rounds in Campaign or Locked In, with separate Sudoku and battle difficulties.

## For creators

The clearest introduction is a short solve-to-battle sequence: place a correct
number, watch its tower grow, attach a module, and start the wave. The same board
then becomes the battlefield. Chest openings and equipment combinations offer
useful follow-up clips. Please identify footage as an alpha development build;
visuals, balance, and controls are still being tested. Steam Deck is the design
focus, but the game does not currently have a Steam Deck Verified rating.
