# Sudoku Defense 0.3.29

- Play the guided first round on the board. Plant two towers, see how their positions shape the enemy route, fit a module, launch a wave, and open the reward chest. You can leave practice at any point and return to your campaign.
- Select **Untimed** in new-run setup to solve at your own pace. Choose **Prepare defense** when your towers are ready. Timed runs retain the countdown and wrong-answer penalties. Existing campaigns keep their original pace.
- Watch the gold route update as you plant. When timed Solve ends, the game explains how to prepare for the wave. During Solve, selecting an empty square takes you straight back to planting from a tower's workshop.
- Select an empty module socket, then choose the module to install. Mouse, touch, and controller players can all equip from the workshop. The tower shows its fitted modules and remaining space.
- Open **Settings** from the title screen or pause menu. Desktop players can choose windowed, borderless fullscreen, or exclusive fullscreen. The game remembers the setting on that device.

The separate one-wave demo supports guided practice and fresh-board replays. Demo progress uses its own profile.
